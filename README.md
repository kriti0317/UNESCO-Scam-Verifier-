Scam Verifier is a web platform designed to help Nepali families verify manpower agencies, education consultancies, foreign universities, and offer letters before making payments. It combines trusted databases, AI-based information extraction, fuzzy matching, and a fixed rule engine to provide transparent and explainable verdicts.

The project was developed as part of the UNESCO Youth Hackathon 2026, under the theme “Play Your Part: Youth Designing the Future of Media and Information Literacy.” It aims to promote safer digital decision-making and help communities identify misleading or fraudulent information.

Key Features:

Agency, consultancy, and university verification
Offer letter scam-risk analysis
Rule-based and explainable verdicts
Scam reporting
Database-grounded chatbot
Shareable verification results

Our goal is to help people make safer decisions and reduce financial loss caused by scams.
